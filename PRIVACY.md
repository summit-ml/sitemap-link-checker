# Privacy policy

Sitemap Link Checker does not collect, store, sell or share any user data.

- **No data leaves your browser.** The extension has no server and no analytics. It makes no requests to Summit ML or to any third party.
- **What it requests.** When you run a check, the extension requests the sitemap and the pages it lists from the site you are on, the same way your browser would if you opened them. On opening, it also reads that site's `robots.txt` to find the sitemap.
- **What it keeps.** The results of a run live in the page's memory and are gone when you close or reload the tab. A CSV file is saved to your computer only when you click **Download CSV**.
- **Passwords and sign-ins.** The extension uses your browser's existing session for the site you are testing. It never asks for, reads or stores passwords.
- **Access.** The extension can only act on a tab after you click its toolbar button on that tab. It has no standing access to any site.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/summit-ml/sitemap-link-checker/issues
